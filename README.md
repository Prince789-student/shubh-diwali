## ✨ Features & Highlights

### 1. 🚀 Real Festive Firecracker & Cracker Simulation Engine
- **💣 Sutli Bomb (सुतली बम / Hydro Bomb)**: Sizzling fuse, blinding screen flash, expanding shockwave ring, billowing smoke & room-shaking sub-bass explosion.
- **🐓 Murga Chhap (मुर्गा छाप पटाखा)**: Iconic Indian red firecrackers with 12 rapid bursts, flying red paper confetti physics & snappy cracks.
- **🌶️ Bullet Bomb (बुलेट बम)**: High-velocity twin crack blasts.
- **🌌 12-Shot Multi-Color Sky Shot (स्काई शॉट)**: Sequential aerial shells bursting into weeping willow and multi-color glittering bouquets.
- **🏺 Shahi Anaar (शाही अनार)**: 20-foot tall volcanic golden fountain with continuous sizzling audio.
- **🌀 Zameeni Chakri (ज़मीनी चकरी)**: High-speed spinning wheel of sparks on the ground.
- **✨ Phuljhadi (जादुई फुलझड़ी)**: Interactive sparkler wand following touch & cursor.
- **🧨 Patakho ki Ladi (१००० वाली लड़ी)**: Rhythmic chain of continuous explosions.
- **🎆 Bhavya Auto Show**: Choreographed non-stop firework celebration.

### 2. 🪷 Virtual Lakshmi-Ganesh Mandir & Synchronized Aarti
- **Maha Lakshmi Aarti (ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता)**: Full devotional audio synchronized word-for-word with karaoke-style highlighting and instant click-to-seek.
- **Shree Ganesh Aarti (जय गणेश देवा)**: Complete lyrics with synchronized vocal playback.
- **Sacred Puja Actions**: Offer flowers (पुष्पार्पण), ring temple bell (घंटी), blow shankh (शंख नाद), and rotate sacred Aarti thali.

### 3. 🎨 All-in-One Festive Creative Studio
- **Rangoli Studio**: Interactive sand brush, color palette, auto-fill, and PNG export.
- **Greeting Card Maker**: Personalized Diwali greeting cards with custom themes and instant WhatsApp sharing.
- **Festive Photo Frame & DP Maker**: Upload photo and generate royal golden Diwali framed portrait.
- **Shahi Mithai Box**: Interactive sweet box packer with customized gift notes.
- **Akash Deepotsav (Sky Lanterns)**: Write personalized wishes and release glowing lanterns into the night sky.

### 4. 🪔 21-Diya Deepotsav Garden & Shubh Muhurat
- Interactive 21-diya lighting tracker with completion blessing modal.
- Diwali historical significance (Ramayana & Samudra Manthan).
- Panch-Parva guide (Dhanteras to Bhai Dooj) & Shubh Muhurat timings.
- Diwali Trivia Knowledge Quiz.

### 5. 📱 100% Mobile Friendly & Offline Audio Synthesizer
- Built using Web Audio API synthesis for zero asset lag and instant explosive responses.
- Completely responsive layout (320px to 4K displays) with smooth touch gestures.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: Semantic HTML5, CSS3 (Modern Variables, Flexbox, Bento Grid, Backdrop Filter)
- **Graphics & Animation**: HTML5 Canvas (Fireworks physics, Petal animation, Sky Lanterns, Rangoli drawing)
- **Audio Engine**: Custom Web Audio API Acoustic Synthesizer (Oscillators, Biquad Filters, Noise Buffers) & Native HTML5 Audio
